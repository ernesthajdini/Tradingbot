Hard Rules (the tripwires and never-dos)\n\n**Never, at any balance below the stated threshold:**\n- **No bought short-dated OTM options, ever.** Stands on Boyer & Vorkink (options as lotteries), Coval & Shumway, and the $2.1B retail loss concentration (Bryzgalova JF 2023) — the critic's valid hit on Beckmeyer's buyer-dominated aggregate doesn't touch this; Kelly on negative EV is $0. The $25/quarter tuition valve is the only sanctioned exception.\n- **No live single-name CSPs below ~$10-20K, at any milestone.** 30-40% single-name concentration is ~3-5x Kelly at *any* balance (critique C accepted; $5K wheel unlock deleted).\n- **No 0DTE, no market orders, no broker-hopping** (Beckmeyer et al. SSRN 4404704; Ernst & Spatt NBER w29883 — PFOF hidden spread exceeds the visible commission saving for a limit-order seller).\n- **No manually typed orders.** A typed order is a tripwire event in the log, not a judgment call (critique H).\n\n**Tripwires — any one stops live trading, 90 days paper:**\n- Drawdown ≥20% from peak (absolute, any cause). A within-rules correlated max-loss on both positions (~9%) is pre-classified as designed-for: review, not exile (critique D1).\n- Live after-friction expectancy negative after 25 live trades.\n- Two consecutive max-loss events (a 1-lot can't be halved; the only de-risk is zero).\n- **Structural, not self-reported, revenge guard: no order approval within 24h of any realized loss — enforced in the ticket system, checkable from the log** (critique G2 accepted: the person who revenge-sizes won't flag it), plus IBKR precautionary size caps.\n- Trading *operations* >1 hr/week. Dev hours are accounted separately and honestly as investment in a software asset and a skill (critique G3) — if they were trading hours, the plan fails its own €60-120/hr opportunity-cost test in month one.\n\n## 5.
