## 1. The Honest Thesis — where the money actually comes from

At today's size, **the deposits are the alpha and the screener is R&D** — that is the playbook's own verdict (Section 5: the P10–P90 spread from options is ~$450, ~13% of the year's outcome; deposits are the other 87%), and nothing in this build round changes it. At **$1,000–$3,400 (now through mid-2027)** the money is $2,400/yr of deposits plus $60–90 of EUR money-market yield; options contribute $0 by design because everything is paper until the 200-trade gate. At **~$3,400 (2027, live 1-lot)** the honest central case for options is roughly **$0** (Playbook P50), with a P90 of +$100–150/yr — and this round's arithmetic sharpened *where* even that would come from: the live tier's friction math makes it a **conditional, post-spike-only strategy** (at current modeled friction a $2-wide spread needs ~$70 credit, reachable only when short-strike IV is ~60%+), worth perhaps +$20–180 in a VIX-spike year and ~$0 in a calm one, plus a possible +$50–150/yr earnings-condor line *if and only if* one paper season proves the edge survives 8-leg friction. At **$10–20K (2028+)** the wheel/CSP unlock arrives per Playbook M12 — not before, at any milestone. Accordingly, **not one item below adds 2026 P&L, and none claims to.** Their combined value is (a) making the Jan 2027 go-live decision honest instead of vibes (worth $100–250 of avoided drawdown on the playbook's own P10 branch), (b) preventing the one documented retail failure mode this system is exposed to — overriding a screener that looks broken after months of correct silence, a -$130 tripwire event — and (c) positioning the account to actually capture the two premium concentrations a 1-lot retail account can structurally access: post-spike windows and the earnings calendar.
